the role

context

Connelly Partners is a creative advertising agency. I sit between the creative, influencer and paid media teams and look after measurement across seven client brands.

Day to day that means tracking, reporting, data QA and performance analysis, and a lot of conversations with clients and media partners about what the data says.

why it matters

Creative and media decisions get made on campaign data. If the tracking is off or the report is confusing, those decisions rest on bad information. My job is to make sure the numbers are right, and that people understand them.
